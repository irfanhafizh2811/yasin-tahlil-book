## 🎨 Visual Brand Guidelines

### Color Palette (Islamic-Inspired)
- **Primary Green:** #2D5A27 (Islamic peace and nature)
- **Gold Accent:** #D4AF37 (Kaaba covering, Islamic art)
- **Calming Blue:** #1B4B5A (Sky, serenity)
- **Warm Beige:** #F5F3E7 (Parchment, tradition)
- **Pure White:** #FFFFFF (Purity, prayer)

### Typography Guidelines
- **Arabic:** Noto Naskh Arabic, Amiri (serif for tradition)
- **Latin Scripts:** Inter, Source Sans Pro (modern, clean)
- **Decorative:** Islamic calligraphy for headers and titles

### Icon Style
- Minimalist Islamic geometric patterns
- Crescent and star motifs (culturally appropriate)
- Family silhouettes (privacy-respectful)
- Prayer bead counter elements

### Photography Guidelines
- Diverse Muslim families from different regions
- Islamic architectural elements (mosques, patterns)
- Prayer setting imagery (tasteful, respectful)
- Natural lighting, peaceful environments
- No faces in prayer positions (Islamic etiquette)
