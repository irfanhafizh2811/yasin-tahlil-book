## 📚 Knowledge Base

### 🕌 Islamic Features & Usage

#### Memorial Creation
**Q: How do I create a memorial for my deceased loved one?**
**A:** Creating a memorial is a sacred act of remembrance. Follow these steps:
1. Open Tahlil and tap "Create Memorial" 
2. Enter your loved one's name with respect and love
3. Select your relationship (Father, Mother, Grandmother, etc.)
4. Choose the date of passing (optional - helps with 40-day traditions)
5. Add a respectful photo if desired
6. Select prayer type: Tahlil, Yasin, or Fatihah
7. Set privacy level (Family, Community, or Private)
8. Review and create with "Bismillah"

*May Allah grant peace to your departed loved one.*

**Q: What are the different types of Islamic prayers available?**
**A:** Tahlil offers authentic Islamic prayers validated by certified scholars:

- **Tahlil (التهليل):** Traditional "La ilaha illa Allah" recitation, often performed for 40 days after passing
- **Surah Yasin (سورة يس):** Known as the "Heart of the Quran," traditionally recited for the deceased
- **Al-Fatihah (الفاتحة):** The opening chapter of the Quran, fundamental Islamic prayer
- **Custom Duas:** Personal prayers and supplications

All content follows authentic Islamic traditions and regional customs.

**Q: Is the Islamic content authenticated by scholars?**
**A:** Yes, Alhamdulillah. All prayer content has been validated by certified Islamic scholars from multiple schools of thought, including:
- Al-Azhar University (Egypt)
- Islamic University of Medina (Saudi Arabia)
- Darul Uloom (India/Pakistan)
- Universitas Islam Indonesia
- Regional Islamic councils across 20+ countries

#### Family Sharing & Privacy

**Q: How do I invite family members to participate in memorial prayers?**
**A:** Islam emphasizes family unity in prayer. To invite family:
1. Open your memorial
2. Tap "Invite Family" 
3. Choose invitation method:
   - WhatsApp with Islamic message template
   - Email with memorial details
   - SMS with download link
   - Copy direct link for any app
4. Add personal message about why this memorial matters
5. Send with love and respect

Family members will receive a beautiful invitation explaining the memorial's purpose.

**Q: What privacy options respect Islamic family values?**
**A:** Tahlil follows Islamic principles of family privacy:

- **Family Only:** Invited relatives only, full memorial details visible, private conversations
- **Community:** Local Muslim community, memorial visible but names private, prayer participation allowed  
- **Private:** Only you can see, personal reflection and prayer
- **Global:** Anonymous contribution to worldwide Islamic community statistics

You can change privacy settings anytime to honor your family's preferences.

**Q: Can I make memorial prayers for non-Muslims?**
**A:** While Tahlil is designed for Islamic traditions, we respect interfaith families. You can:
- Create private memorials for personal remembrance
- Use general Duas rather than specific Islamic prayers
- Focus on gratitude and positive memories
- Consult with local Islamic scholars for guidance

### 📱 Technical Support

#### App Installation & Setup

**Q: What devices are compatible with Tahlil?**
**A:** Tahlil works on:
- **Android:** 7.0 (API 24) or higher
- **Storage:** 100MB free space recommended
- **Internet:** Required for community features, offline prayer counter available
- **Permissions:** Camera (memorial photos), Notifications (prayer reminders)

The app is optimized for both smartphones and tablets.

**Q: How do I change the app language?**
**A:** Tahlil supports 12 languages with full Islamic cultural adaptation:
1. Open Settings → Language & Region
2. Select your preferred language:
   - العربية (Arabic) - RTL interface
   - English - Global standard
   - Bahasa Indonesia - Southeast Asia customs
   - اردو (Urdu) - South Asian traditions
   - Türkçe - Turkish Islamic culture
   - فارسی (Persian) - Central Asian customs
   - Français, Deutsch, Español - Western communities
   - Kiswahili - African Islamic traditions
   - پښتو (Pashto) - Afghan/Pakistani regions
3. App interface and prayers will update automatically

**Q: The app is loading slowly. What should I do?**
**A:** Performance optimization steps:
1. Close other apps to free memory
2. Check internet connection strength  
3. Restart the app completely
4. Clear app cache: Settings → Storage → Clear Cache
5. Update to latest version from Play Store
6. Restart your device

If issues persist, the prayer counter works offline while we resolve connectivity.

#### Account & Data Management

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I recover my account?**
