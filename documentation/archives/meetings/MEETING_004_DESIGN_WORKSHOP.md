## 🎨 **Design System Framework**

### 🌍 **Global Design Principles**

#### **1. Cultural Respect & Authenticity**
- Islamic geometric patterns as subtle design elements
- Respectful color palettes (avoiding inappropriate colors)
- Authentic Arabic typography for religious content
- Regional adaptation without losing core identity

#### **2. Universal Accessibility**  
- Large, clear typography for elderly users
- High contrast ratios for vision accessibility
- Simple, intuitive navigation patterns
- Voice assistance for low-literacy users

#### **3. Photo-Centricity with Scale**
- Consistent photo treatment across all cultures
- AI enhancement for old/damaged photos
- Respectful framing and presentation
- Privacy controls for sensitive images

#### **4. Social Integration Balance**
- Spiritual focus maintained during social features
- Privacy-first approach to sharing
- Community building without commercialization
- Respectful social media representation

### 🎨 **Visual Design Language**

#### **Color Palette Strategy**
```
Primary Colors (Culturally Neutral):
├── Deep Blue (#1a237e) - Night prayer, trust
├── Warm Gold (#ffd54f) - Light, guidance, reverence
├── Pure White (#ffffff) - Purity, peace
├── Warm Gray (#f5f5f5) - Serenity, reading
└── Soft Green (#4caf50) - Islamic tradition, nature

Regional Adaptations:
├── Middle East: Traditional Islamic greens and golds
├── Southeast Asia: Warmer earth tones
├── South Asia: Richer jewel tones
├── Africa: Natural earth and sunset colors
└── Western Countries: Subtle, modern adaptations
```

#### **Typography System**
```
Arabic Script:
├── Primary: Authentic Quranic font for sacred text
├── Secondary: Modern Arabic for UI elements
├── Sizing: Large, readable, culturally appropriate

Latin Script:
├── Primary: Clean, modern sans-serif for UI
├── Secondary: Serif for longer reading content
├── Localized: Fonts optimized for each language

Special Considerations:
├── RTL/LTR text mixing and alignment
├── Dynamic sizing for accessibility
├── Cultural font preferences by region
└── Calligraphy elements for spiritual sections
```
