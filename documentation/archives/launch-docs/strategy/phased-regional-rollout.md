# 🌍 Tahlil Global Launch Strategy - Phased Regional Rollout

## 🎯 Launch Philosophy

**Islamic Community-First Approach:** Our launch strategy prioritizes authentic Islamic community validation, cultural sensitivity, and gradual adoption across diverse Muslim regions worldwide. Each phase builds trust, validates cultural appropriateness, and ensures sustainable growth rooted in Islamic values.

---

## 📊 Overview: Four-Phase Global Rollout

### Phase Distribution Strategy
```
Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Core Islamic Markets - 4 Countries
├─ Focus: Islamic authenticity validation

```
